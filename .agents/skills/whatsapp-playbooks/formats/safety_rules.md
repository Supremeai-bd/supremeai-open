# Safety Rules

These are execution rules, not research notes. Apply them before any account read, write, publish, outreach, device, or Business workflow.

## Enforcement detail

1. **Stay bounded and policy-compliant**: no unsolicited messaging, no burst automation, no concurrent account actions, and no behavior intended to evade limits or detection.
2. **Read-only first**: Cards 1 and 5 (digest, extraction) are zero-write end to end; opening a chat means scrolling and reading, nothing more.
3. **Every send confirmed individually**:
   - Draft mode A: Computer Use types the draft into the target chat's input box and then **stops**; the user presses send.
   - A send to "Message yourself" is still a send and requires confirmation.
   - Scheduled assistance creates a draft and reminder; it does not perform an unattended CU send.
4. **Bulk is not a Desktop CU workflow**. Ordinary broadcast lists are mobile-only and subject to the 256-recipient and saved-contact requirements. The WhatsApp Business app also has a distinct rollout-limited business-broadcast feature with consent, opt-out, review, pricing, and scheduling rules. A Business Platform integration is a third route with its own runtime and delivery contracts; it is not a substitute name for either app feature.
5. **Always refuse, and always offer an alternative**: rotating multiple accounts, third-party bulk senders, buying followers, artificial engagement, and reverse-engineered personal-account automation. Offer the official route that actually fits the account and region: ordinary mobile broadcast-list guidance, the detected WhatsApp Business app business-broadcast feature, or a separately verified Business Platform workflow. Never imply that Desktop/Web can execute a phone-only flow or that public Platform capability proves a Manus runtime.
6. **Privacy boundary**: open only the agreed chats and time range, keep read-only work write-free, avoid unrelated conversations, and use a user-selected output location. Do not claim host-only processing. Respect any product privacy restriction and never work around it.
